# Operational Briefs Playbook

## Purpose

An **Operational Brief** is a high-density deliverable designed to maximize signal, eliminate narrative fluff, and translate complex geopolitical events, technical workflows, or multi-variable systems into actionable baseline awareness in under 60 seconds.

---

## Context & Prerequisites

* **Required Tools:** LLM / text editor, primary source documentation, OSINT/data feeds.
* **System State:** Input data or narrative text is available for distillation.
* **Reference Playbooks:** `briefs-playbook.md`, `conventions-playbook.md`.

---

## The Protocol (The "How-To")

1. **Extract Core Mechanics:** Strip out dramatic hooks, rhetorical setups, and speculative political intent. Identify the physical inputs, constraints, trade loops, or resource diversions.
2. **Draft the Executive Signal (TL;DR):** Write 1–2 sentences summarizing the primary operational shift. Focus strictly on system mechanics.
3. **Format Systemic/Operational Mechanics:** Create 3–4 concise bullet points detailing functional changes (e.g., asset reallocation, cost shifts, physical bottlenecks).
4. **Build the Narrativised Bibliography:** Select 2–4 targeted primary sources, legal frameworks, or historical precedents. Add a brief annotation to each explaining its exact role in the system.
5. **Verify Word Count & Signal Density:** Ensure total length remains under 250 words and contains zero introductory meta-announcements.

---

## Standards & Patterns

* **Zero Fluff:** Avoid introductory setup phrases (e.g., "Here is a breakdown of..."). Jump directly into the structured sections.
* **No Labeled Conclusions:** Do not add "In Summary" or "Conclusion" headers at the end.
* **Structural Rigor:** Maintain the mandatory 3-part taxonomy:
* **TL;DR**
* **Operational Breakdown** (Bulleted)
* **Narrativised Bibliography**



---

## Validation (Acceptance Criteria)

* The brief can be scanned and fully comprehended in under 60 seconds.
* Total length is strictly under 250 words.
* All narrative filler is removed, leaving only physical/systemic constraints and data hooks.
* The bibliography links directly to verifiable historical, legal, or technical data.

---

## Maintenance

Update this playbook if new structural requirements for high-density intelligence delivery are introduced or if output signal drops below the 60-second comprehension threshold.
